The methodologyFORGE — Intent-Driven Development

Most failed AI-assisted development isn't a model-capability problem — it's an intent-clarity problem. FORGE makes intent expression a first-class engineering activity, with phases, deliverables, and quality gates. You don't earn the right to generate code until you can describe what done looks like.

F

Focus

Capture intent: what, why, and success in testable terms.

O

Orchestrate

Decompose intent into work AI and humans can carry.

R

Refine

Make it verifiable: acceptance criteria, given-when-then.

G

Generate

Now the code — by agent, pair, or hand — traced to intent.

E

Evaluate

Prove outcomes match intent, with evidence on record.

Intent

The precise articulation of what you're building and why it matters — sharp enough for an agent with no intuition, only instructions.

Outcome

Intent without a measurable outcome is a wish. Every line of code traces to an outcome; every outcome traces to an intent.

Accountability

The verifiable chain from intent through outcome to evidence — what makes autonomous development trustworthy.
