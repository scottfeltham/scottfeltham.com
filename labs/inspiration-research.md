# Portfolio/personal-brand design research (2026-07-19)

> Agent-researched. Full citations were in the run; key findings condensed here.

## The two lanes on the award circuit

1. **Technical spectacle** (WebGL/3D nav/shaders — Hiroto Sato hirotos.com, Medhat
   Alkadri's orbital nav, Ricardo Chance) — optimizes for craft-demo/client work.
2. **Restrained monochrome/typographic craft** (Dmitry Glukhovsky's progress-bar
   motif, Haoqi Wen's monochrome + live clock, Sebastian Wittig's brown/near-black
   + yellow accent) — the lane that matches an authority goal.

No current winner uses warm-black + saturated orange; census skews monochrome or
near-black + neutral/metallic accent.

## The thought-leader tier (Scott's real peer group)

Fowler, Abramov (overreacted.io), Willison, Larson (lethain), Uncle Bob: near-zero
visual design, writing-first, plainness as rhetoric ("the ideas already have
currency"). Exceptions correlate with audience-building (Orosz, Kent Beck — more
designed because the newsletter is a product; Dave Thomas — personal-story-led).
**Implication:** launching FORGE/IDD justifies more designed identity than Fowler
needs, but the ornamentation ceiling is low; writing/methodology should be the
first-class citizen as essays appear.

## Signals & tells (2026)

- **Inter at default weight + blue accent + card grid = the named "AI-generated"
  tell** (Muzli). Serif display = bespoke signal. Typewolf premium rankings favor
  distinctive grotesques (Apercu/GT America/Founders) or serif-display pairings.
- **Dark+teal-on-navy** (Brittany Chiang formula) = most-forked template look; avoid.
- **Glassmorphism at scale** took a credibility hit (NN/g "Liquid Glass Is
  Cracked"); only surgical small-overlay use survives.
- **Subtraction is the premium move** — Obys/BL/S® 2026 rebuilds removed nav/UI
  ("menus became less visible, then unnecessary").
- **One signature interaction beats a battery** — winners each have exactly one
  memorable device.
- Maximalist-personality sites (Wattenberger, Cassie Evans) have retreated to
  plainness or exited — playful-illustrated is the wrong long-term identity for
  an ideas-authority.
- GSAP ScrollTrigger still standard at the top — but narrative-functional motion
  only.
- Ember orange verdict: **safe-but-unremarkable, not fatigued** (inference —
  follow-up quota died; re-verify if it matters). Differentiate via execution.

## Application to scottfeltham.com

- Keep serif display (asset). Consider distinctive grotesque for body copy.
- Consolidate motion to ONE signature: the INTENT particle interlude (thesis-tied).
  Hero glyph-scramble duplicates the same noise→clarity idea — simplify hero.
- Audit chrome: the glass island nav is borderline vs the anti-glass finding —
  candidate for simplification.
- Palette: of the five lab directions, **Verdigris** (pine + brass) matches the
  cited premium pattern (near-black + restrained metallic accent, cf. Wittig)
  and the literary-authority tone; **Gallium** is the standout-by-contrast light
  option; **Signal** is the purest craft-lane play. Riso/Phosphor sit in the
  personality lane the research warns about.
- IA roadmap: as FORGE essays ship (authority plan), writing becomes the
  homepage's first-class citizen, Fowler-style — design recedes as reputation
  compounds.
