Scroll-driven video, three ways

Same on-brand generated clip (960×540, 6s, all-keyframe encoded, 277 KB) exercised through the three production techniques. Scroll slowly, then fast; try reversing. Verdicts at the bottom of each.

01 · currentTime scrub

scroll position → video.currentTime, with lerp smoothing · the "Apple AirPods" feel with a real <video>

t = 0.00s

How: ScrollTrigger maps section progress to a target time; a per-frame lerp eases currentTime toward it (never set it raw — it stutters). The catch: seeking smoothness depends entirely on encoding — this clip is encoded with keyframe interval 1 (-g 1), which makes every frame seekable but ~3× the file size. Default-encoded video will judder badly. iOS needs muted playsinline and can still hiccup on long clips. Verdict: best effort-to-wow ratio for clips under ~10s.

02 · canvas frame scrub

scroll → frame index drawn to canvas · what Apple actually ships (image sequences) — here rendered procedurally, zero assets

frame 0

How: scroll progress selects a frame; the frame is painted to canvas. Production sites pre-export 60–150 JPEG/WebP frames and blit them — perfectly smooth in both directions, no codec seeking at all. This demo goes one further: the "video" is computed live (a flow-field of agents converging from noise into the word INTENT — the site's thesis as motion), so there are no assets and it's resolution-independent. Verdict: the most reliable scrub; the choice when the motion must be flawless or generative.

03 · cinematic reveal

video plays itself; scroll drives scale/radius/parallax of the frame · cheap, robust, still feels expensive

a small window —

How: the video just loops; ScrollTrigger scrubs the container — scale from 38vw to full-bleed, corner radius to 0, caption crossfade. No seeking, so encoding doesn't matter and iOS is happy. Verdict: the pragmatic default — 90% of the perceived value at 10% of the risk. Ideal wrapper for a real hero film later (e.g. a generated abstract or a screen-capture of the agentic pipeline at work).
